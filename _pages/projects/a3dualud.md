<h1>A3-DualUD: Source-free Unsupervised Domain Adaptation via Anatomical Anchor Alignment and Dual-path Uncertainty Denoising for Cross-modality Medical Image Segmentation</h1>

<p>
Jie Zhou<sup>*</sup>, <strong>Yulong Shi</strong><sup>*</sup>, Lin Qi, Xue Jiang, Shouliang Qi, Wei Qian. (<sup>*</sup>equal contribution)<br />
<em>Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine</em>, 2025. <span style="color:red;"><b>IF: 4.8, JCR Q1</b></span>
</p>

<h2>Abstract</h2>
<p>
  A3-DualUD proposes a source-free unsupervised domain adaptation method for cross-modality medical image segmentation. The approach combines anatomical anchor alignment with dual-path uncertainty denoising to address domain shift challenges without requiring access to source domain data during adaptation.
</p>

<h2>Method Overview</h2>
<p>
  The framework employs two key components: (1) anatomical anchor alignment that leverages anatomical structure priors to guide domain alignment, and (2) dual-path uncertainty denoising that reduces noise in adaptation through complementary pathways. This combination enables effective source-free domain adaptation for medical image segmentation across different imaging modalities.
</p>
