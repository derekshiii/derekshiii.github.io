<h1>HEAL: Learning-Free Source Free Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for Cross-Modality Medical Image Segmentation</h1>

<p>
<strong>Yulong Shi</strong>, Jiapeng Li, Lin Qi.<br />
<em>The 36th British Machine Vision Conference</em> (BMVC), 2025. <span style="color:red;"><b>CCF-C, CORE A</b></span>
</p>

<h2>Abstract</h2>
<p>
  HEAL introduces a learning-free approach to source-free unsupervised domain adaptation for cross-modality medical image segmentation. The method eliminates the need for source data access during adaptation, providing an efficient solution for deploying segmentation models across different medical imaging modalities.
</p>

<h2>Method Overview</h2>
<p>
  The framework achieves domain adaptation without any additional training or fine-tuning. By leveraging intrinsic properties of medical images and domain-specific priors, HEAL enables direct deployment of pre-trained segmentation models to new imaging modalities while maintaining competitive performance.
</p>
