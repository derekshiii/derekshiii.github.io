<h1>Tell2Adapt: A Unified Framework for Source Free Unsupervised Domain Adaptation via Vision Foundation Model</h1>

<p>
<b>Yulong Shi</b>, Shijie Li, Ziyi Li, Lin Qi.<br />
<em>Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)</em>, June 2026. <span style="color:red;"><b>CCF-A, CORE A*</b></span>
</p>

<h2>Abstract</h2>
<p>
  Tell2Adapt presents a unified framework for source-free unsupervised domain adaptation (SFUDA) leveraging vision foundation models. The approach enables effective domain adaptation without accessing source data, utilizing the rich representations of large-scale pretrained models to bridge domain gaps across diverse tasks.
</p>

<h2>Method Overview</h2>
<p>
  The framework exploits the generalization capability of vision foundation models to perform adaptation in a source-free setting. By leveraging the intrinsic knowledge embedded in these models, Tell2Adapt achieves robust cross-domain performance without requiring access to the original training data.
</p>
