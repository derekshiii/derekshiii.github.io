On July 22, 2025, **Ziyi Li** and I won **First Prize** (graduate research group) at the **10th National Biomedical Engineering Innovation Design Competition**.

Our project is **SFLocNet：基于无源无监督解剖先验引导的跨模态医学锚点定位框架**. We worked on **medical image landmark localization** across modalities, including **X-ray skull** and **chest** landmark points.

Special thanks to our advisor **Li Qi** for the support and guidance.
