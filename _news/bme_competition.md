## Competition Overview

On July 22, 2025, I participated in the **10th National Biomedical Engineering Innovation Design Competition** held at Xi'an Jiaotong University, Xi'an, Shaanxi Province.

Our project **"SFLocNet: Source-Free Anatomical Landmark Localization Framework Based on Cross-Modality Medical Image Analysis"** won the **First Prize** in the graduate research group.

---

## About the Competition

The National Biomedical Engineering Innovation Design Competition is one of the most prestigious academic competitions for biomedical engineering students in China. It aims to:

- Promote innovation and practical skills in biomedical engineering
- Foster collaboration between academia and industry
- Showcase cutting-edge research in medical imaging, AI-assisted diagnosis, and medical devices

This year's competition attracted participants from top universities across China, making this achievement particularly meaningful.

---

## Our Project

Our project focuses on developing a novel framework for anatomical landmark localization that can adapt to new imaging modalities without requiring access to source domain data. This is particularly important for:

- **Cross-modality medical image analysis**: Enabling models trained on one imaging modality (e.g., CT) to work on another (e.g., MRI)
- **Privacy preservation**: Source data is not needed during adaptation, addressing data privacy concerns
- **Clinical applicability**: Reducing the need for extensive labeled data in new clinical settings
