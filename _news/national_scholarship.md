## Award Announcement

On October 27, 2025, I was honored to receive the **National Scholarship**, the highest level of scholarship awarded to graduate students in China.

---

## About the National Scholarship

The National Scholarship is established by the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China to recognize outstanding graduate students who demonstrate:

- **Academic Excellence**: Outstanding academic performance and research achievements
- **Research Innovation**: Significant contributions to scientific research
- **Comprehensive Quality**: Strong overall development in academic and personal aspects

The scholarship is highly competitive, with only a small percentage of graduate students receiving this prestigious award each year.
