## Conference Overview

On November 23-25, 2025, I attended the **International Conference on AI Scientists (ICAIS 2025)** held at Zhongguancun Academy in Beijing, China. This prestigious conference featured **multiple Nobel Prize laureates** as keynote speakers.

---

## About ICAIS 2025

The International Conference on AI Scientists (ICAIS 2025) is a premier academic conference bringing together leading researchers, scholars, and practitioners in the field of artificial intelligence.

### Organizing Institutions

**Guidance Units:**
- Beijing Municipal Education Commission
- Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission
- Zhongguancun Science Park Management Committee
- Beijing Municipal Human Resources Bureau

**Host Institutions:**
- Zhongguancun Academy
- Zhongguancun Institute of Artificial Intelligence
- Tsinghua University
- Westlake University

**Co-organizer:**
- University of Chicago

**Academic Support:**
- Chinese Academy of Sciences
- Peking University
- Institute of Automation, CAS
- Beihang University
- Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence
- Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance
- Shanghai AI Lab

---

## Conference Highlights

The conference featured:

- **Keynote speeches from Nobel Prize laureates**, including world-renowned scientists in physics and chemistry
- Technical sessions covering machine learning, computer vision, NLP, and AI for healthcare
- Poster presentations and demonstrations
- Networking opportunities with leading AI scientists

During the conference, I had the honor of:

- Presenting my poster on medical image analysis research
- Meeting and obtaining a signature from **Prof. Konstantin Novoselov**, the 2010 Nobel Prize laureate in Physics for his groundbreaking work on graphene
- Learning about the latest advances in AI research
- Connecting with researchers working on related topics in medical AI

This experience has been instrumental in broadening my research perspective and establishing connections with the broader AI research community.
